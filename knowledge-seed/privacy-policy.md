Source: https://example.com/privacy (copied by the operator).

Last updated: September 2026. Specifically applies to this outreach automation system.

## 1. Client-Side Analysis & Privacy

Draft analysis and visualizers run in your local web browser. Sample drafts and personal notes are kept strictly confidential and secure.

## 2. Access & Account Information

If you submit your email address for access or account administration, we collect solely necessary operational data. This information is used strictly to:

- Administer system accounts and send sequence dispatches.
- Notify you of critical system operations and health alerts.
- Ensure system deliverability and domain alignment.

## 3. Zero Spam & No Unauthorized Third-Party Sharing

We will never sell, rent, license, or exchange contact details with data brokers, advertisers, or third parties.

## 4. Local Storage & Cookies

This system utilizes secure signed cookies and minimal browser local storage strictly to manage session state and user preferences.

## 5. Unsubscribing & Data Deletion

You maintain total control. Contacts can unsubscribe at any time, or email privacy@example.com to request data purging.
