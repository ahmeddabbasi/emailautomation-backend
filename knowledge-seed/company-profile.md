# Outreach System: company profile

Source: company reference documentation, copied by the operator. Everything below is what the company says about itself.

## What the Platform is

The platform is an AI-powered content and outreach management system. It learns a person's writing voice from their existing writing and generates content that sounds authentic and human. It checks drafts for reach risk before sending or publishing, and it schedules and dispatches through official APIs.

Tagline: "An AI that learns your voice and handles your outreach." Positioning line: "Write human. Communicate smart."

The product launched in 2026, built with high quality in public.

## The problem it addresses

Modern engagement algorithms and spam filters demote generic, templated content: generic AI openers, engagement bait, corporate jargon, hashtag stacks, and flat sentence rhythm. The signals that matter are dwell time, replies, and genuine interactions. Content flagged as generic stops travelling and lands in spam or promotions.

## Features

Voice DNA
- The user pastes three writing samples (emails, posts, or notes). That is the whole setup.
- The product analyzes the writing style: average sentence length, openers, punctuation habits, and vocabulary preferences.
- Every voice version is kept and the user can roll back.

Reach Risk Check
- Audits drafts against common spam and robotic patterns: generic AI openers, engagement bait, corporate jargon, and AI pattern density.
- Runs directly in the browser. The draft is not transmitted unnecessarily.

Substance, not just style
- A knowledge base of the user's real numbers, outcomes, and facts is pulled into generation, ensuring high relevance and substantive communication.

Scheduling and publishing
- Automated drip dispatching and sequence pacing.
- Scheduled according to recipient time zones and working hours.

## Who it is for

Founders, consultants, agencies, and revenue teams who conduct outbound outreach and communication.

## Trust and security

- Uses official APIs and secure webhooks.
- Writing samples are encrypted, isolated, and never used to train public models.
- The user can disconnect at any time.

The first 100 accounts get a founding price that stays locked for as long as you stay subscribed.
